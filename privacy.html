Privacy policy

  • In force from: 2026-10-03
  • Who this is about: the household that operates Zero. There are no other users.

What Zero is

Zero is a desktop application that runs on one machine in one household. It is not a service, it is not hosted, and it has no accounts to sign up for. Nobody operates it on anyone else's behalf.

It exists to plan the operator's own debt payoff. The only person whose financial data it holds is the operator and their household.

What it collects

Only what the operator enters or explicitly connects:

  • Debts: balance, interest rate, minimum payment, due date, credit limit, promotional terms.
  • Income and spending, either typed in or imported from statements the operator provides.
  • Transactions, balances, minimum payments, interest rates and due dates for accounts the operator chooses to connect through the account aggregator.

It does not collect anything about anyone else, and it has no way to.

Where that data goes

Nowhere, except where the operator sends it.

  • It is stored on the operator's own machine, in a directory outside the application's source code, encrypted at rest.
  • It is never uploaded to the developer, who is also the operator. There is no server to upload to.
  • There is no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, and no third-party script in the application.

Exactly three kinds of traffic leave the machine, and only these:

  1. The account aggregator (Plaid), for accounts the operator explicitly connects, under the operator's own agreement with that company. Their privacy policy governs what they hold.
  2. Institution websites, where the operator signs in on the institution's own page during linking. That page is not hosted or scripted by Zero, and Zero cannot read what is typed on it.
  3. Font files from a public CDN, which carry no personal data.

Consent

Nothing is collected without an explicit act: typing a figure, importing a file, or completing an institution's own linking flow. Connecting an account requires completing the aggregator's consent screen, which states what will be shared. No account is connected by default, and no account is connected automatically.

A rate change reported by an aggregator is shown for the operator to accept rather than applied, because applying it silently would change the payoff plan around a number the operator never saw.

Credentials

Zero never holds a bank or lender password. Linking goes through the aggregator's own flow. Aggregator keys and access tokens are held in the operating system's credential store, never in Zero's own files.

Keeping and deleting

See the separate retention policy. In short: data is kept until the operator deletes it, there is no background copy anywhere, and one command removes everything — disconnecting each institution at the aggregator first, so the connection stops as well as the file.

Rights

As the only subject of this data is the operator, the usual rights of access, correction, export and erasure are exercised directly: the data is a set of files in a directory they control, the application can display and edit all of it, and one command deletes it.
